# Mahdi Mostajabdaveh

**Principal OR/AI Scientist at Kinaxis · AI research and technical leadership**

I develop AI systems that reason about complex problems and discover better algorithms, and I lead their path from research to production. My work connects language models, multi-agent systems, and mathematical optimization.

At Kinaxis, I lead AI acceleration for a production optimization solver and the evaluation infrastructure for coding agents that modify it. Previously, as Senior Staff Researcher and AI Lead for Huawei's OptVerse solver, I led AI-for-optimization research, helped deliver a routing engine used by more than 20 enterprise customers, and developed OptvEvolve for LLM-driven algorithm evolution.
